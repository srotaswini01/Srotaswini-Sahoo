Know me

Diligent learner

Hi there! I'm Srotaswini (AKA Sro), a UX Designer with experience building consumer-facing and B2B SaaS products in fast-paced environments. I aspire to create experiences that make people's lives a bit easier.

Over the years, I've designed experiences across multiple domains — ed-tech, enterprise, telecom, healthcare — and have worked in different environments: early-stage start-ups, a design firm, and MNCs.

Reflecting on my life, I see my journey as a continuous learning experience. In every phase, whether during my college days, my travel adventures, or living with my parents during the pandemic, I have learned something valuable. Observing people's behaviour in various situations has taught me many lessons.

It's been a joy to witness my own growth. I have seen many versions of myself, and I love each new update.

"Learn continually — there's always 'one more thing' to learn!"

Learn from everyone

I love to travel, meet new people and learn something from them — because everyone knows something that you don't.

Every little detail matters

I take joy in making small changes and seeing how they create a significant impact.

Culture drives value

I strive to foster a positive environment, and love seeing people come together to solve problems and create value.

Well, a little bit more about me!

I believe that design should be used to solve problems and make people's lives better. I'm always looking for new ways to push the boundaries of what is possible and to create experiences that are both meaningful and memorable.

When I'm not designing, you can find me searching for my next book to read, exploring new travel destinations, meeting new people, or organising my home. I love documenting my journey and writing about my feelings, emotions, and the small things that matter to me.

I am fascinated by the evolution of technology and its influence on communication, learning, and thinking. The ever-changing needs of people and their environments continually present challenges that demand innovative solutions. As a product designer, I am grateful to be in a field where I can address the everyday problems people face.
