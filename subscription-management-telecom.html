Overview

This telecommunication company aims to develop a subscription management system to streamline and improve the handling of subscription-related actions. Currently, these tasks are managed manually using Excel and email, which can lead to errors. To mitigate these issues, our goal is to design an intuitive and efficient application that enables the recording and tracking of all subscriptions.

Business Goal

As an outcome, they aim to eliminate revenue leakage in their subscription business by implementing an efficient and secure subscription management system.

Design Goal

The primary goal is to streamline workflows, understanding various user roles to achieve their goals efficiently and effectively.

  • Reduce time taken for each task
  • Automate tasks & boost productivity
  • Simplify user experience

The problem statement

To find the right solution, it's crucial to thoroughly understand the problem from all perspectives. Identifying the correct problem is essential before jumping into solutions.

The problem

Managing subscriptions manually, including reminders and renewals, created chaos and frequent errors. The process involved multiple people and dependencies, causing delays and making the system inefficient and cumbersome.

They faced challenges managing subscription data, with no consolidated view of active, expiring, terminated, or suspended contracts. The lack of visibility of crucial information led to revenue leakage as users were not able to make informed decisions. Additionally, the manual process disrupted communication, negatively impacting customer relationships and service consistency. A streamlined, automated solution is essential to reduce errors, improve efficiency, and provide a reliable experience.

This was happening because the absence of an integrated system prevented real-time tracking and clear communication of subscription statuses, which was leading to revenue leakage.

Understanding context

We recognised that managing subscription data is complex due to its unique nature and the non-standard process for changing subscription statuses. Each user type has distinct roles and access levels, and also the large volume of data requires careful prioritisation and understanding.

Currently, excessive data access for everyone is causing confusion and overwhelming users. Not all data is relevant to everyone, so we needed to ensure users see only the information pertinent to their needs.

Inefficiencies due to manual data entry and lack of integration.

The use of email for handling updates, reports, and billing results in a disjointed workflow.

How is the work getting done right now?

Operators initiate requests via email and maintain data using Excel. They check information in the spreadsheet, handle updates manually, and track payments. Reports and bills are sent and processed through email, leading to a fragmented and error-prone system.

But again, the loophole is that often no one knows whom to contact or who updated certain data, which leads to chaos and confusion.

We wanted to understand the diverse personas our design needed to address, focusing on their daily routines, challenges, and needs. This approach helped us align the experience with their real-world workflows, while ensuring the design remained consistent and didn't deviate significantly for each persona.

Ideation and solutions

Began by understanding the problem, user needs, and pain points to identify the design solutions required. Then, created the architecture to establish a solid foundation for the design.

How might we streamline manual processes to reduce errors and improve efficiency in contract management?
How might we enhance visibility of data and help them in decision-making?
How might we automate routine tasks to minimise manual workload and free up their time to increase productivity?
Our goal: things to achieve in the journey
Creating the base
  • The IA was designed to be straightforward, with simplicity and ease of use as top priorities.
  • Given the role-based access, each persona needed to perform common tasks effortlessly. We carefully mapped out all tasks to streamline the experience.
  • Scalability was also a key consideration, ensuring that the platform could accommodate future features and functionalities.
  • For Admin, the primary focus is creating and managing base tables.
  • Operators and managers concentrate on viewing and managing contracts.
  • Daily tasks include activating, suspending, extending, or terminating contracts, and accessing reports for informed decision-making.
Our goal
I understood the tasks each persona needs to perform and recognised that the home page dashboard is crucial. It provides an overview of everything and facilitates access to other tasks.
The system should aid in decision-making and simplify users' work, rather than overwhelming them with excessive data. The goal is to help users track opportunities effectively and maximise their potential for growth.
Scalability is crucial for a large organisation where data will grow over time. As this is an MVP, the focus is on building a strong foundation that can adapt to a rapidly evolving business and accommodate a diverse user base.

Visual design

Began with wireframes, iterated extensively, and incorporated client feedback to ensure the experience, purpose, and functionality were optimised.

The solution

With a lot of numbers involved, I focused on understanding the priority, choosing only the most important details for decision-making.

We aimed to create a simple, easy-to-read dashboard, especially since it's their first time using it. Instead of making them scan through all 64 pieces of information for each contract, I wanted to simplify their experience and make their tasks easier.

“We were so focused on tasks like activation, extension, and renewal that we never realised how much we needed an overview until now.”

- User Feedback

Overview: Subscriptions, Revenue, and Opportunities

The graphs are simple and straightforward, designed to be understood at a glance without taking up too much time. Since revenue and opportunity are key factors, we've made them a priority in the UI.

The graphs made it easy to visualise and compare important data. The consolidated segments of Accounts, Revenue, and Opportunities provided a clear snapshot of the situation at a glance, while still allowing us to dive deeper if needed.

- User Response
Admin

Admin can go to any of these contract types and create a new contract by entering all the data. They can also view all these contracts in a tabular view.

Manager

The Manager will use the same navigation system as the Admin, but with different functionality. While the Manager can perform all necessary actions, they'll also have a dedicated "Pending Requests" section to view and manage requests by type.

Operator

For the Operator, the navigation is similar to the Manager's, but with limited actions. The Operator can only send requests for contract renewal and termination. But they can take other actions of their own.

The solution

Simplifying the process for users to take contract actions by allowing them to quickly scan the necessary information.

Initially, users spent almost 20 minutes searching for a specific contract and reviewing details to determine the next action. By prioritising key data and possible actions, we streamlined the process, significantly reducing the time needed to find and act on contracts.

Idea 1

Contextual CTAs could lead to confusion, forcing users to read them repeatedly, which consumes unnecessary time.

Idea 2

Not all actions can be upfront, as it may overwhelm users. They need to review all the details before making any significant decisions.

Idea 3

View, edit, and delete will be the most frequently used actions, and users shouldn't need to go through all the details to perform them. Icon buttons make these actions quick to recognise. More important actions can be taken once the user reviews the contract details.

Table structure: only necessary information

After several iterations, we focused on displaying only the essential information upfront. No horizontal scrolling or dropdown menus were used; instead, a new page was created for users to thoroughly view the details. Additionally, bulk actions were introduced for easy deletion and export of contracts.

To ensure the manager's attention is drawn to important areas requiring action, we highlighted these elements clearly in our design.

We grouped relevant data, enhancing readability and making it easier and quicker to grasp the information, ultimately saving nearly 13 minutes.

The approval process is now simpler and easier to track. As for actions like renewals and extensions, managers need to approve them.

Impact of our solution

Reduced time spent on each contract action by 75%.

With the old method, they were taking almost 18 minutes to read the details and take actions like renew, termination, extension or suspension.

Minimised errors.

By minimising errors, we reduced revenue leakage and the need for follow-up conversations, saving users time and energy and decreasing frustration.

Simplified comparison of annual and monthly data through the dashboard.

This streamlined interface enhances user efficiency and decision-making by presenting information in an accessible and actionable format.

Achieved almost 100% user satisfaction

Users reported no issues after the redesign. Considering they previously managed everything manually, this is a significant improvement for them.
